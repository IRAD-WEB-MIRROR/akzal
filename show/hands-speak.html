Hands Speak

Gulsara Toichu

Paintings and a wall of 2,300 signs. Gulsara Toichu (b. 1994), deaf painter and teacher, compiled the first dictionary of Iradi Sign Language (2021). Here the dictionary becomes a room: every sign drawn by hand on the wall, and twenty-two new paintings of hands, horses and the Talas valley.

Guided tours in Iradi Sign Language every Saturday at 12:00, with the Iyık Janyl school for the deaf.
